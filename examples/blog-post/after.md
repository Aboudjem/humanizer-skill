Stable Diffusion 1.4 was released on 22 August 2022. Since then, anyone with a GPU that has 10 GB of VRAM can run an AI image generator at home, whether they trained as an artist or have never held a brush.

That access is the real change, and it's easy to oversell. The tools are simpler to use, but simple isn't the same as skilled. A beginner still has to learn what makes an image good, and an experienced artist still has to decide what is worth making.

The technology has also caused real disputes, and they haven't stopped people from using it. The creative community is still working, arguing and experimenting. I wouldn't predict where that ends up.

What I do expect is more collaboration between people and machines, in forms nobody has settled on yet. People will keep wanting to make things and show them to each other. That part hasn't changed.
